# Use AI helpers: Basics

You can use TribeCode with no AI at all, and add help when you want it.

## Three kinds of help

- **Flint.** The guide built into the app. He answers questions, runs steps and starts things from a sentence. He works with no AI and no account.
- **Your own AI helper.** An AI you already use, such as Claude, Cursor, Codex, ChatGPT or Gemini. You bring it in and it works next to you.
- **A model on this device.** An optional free model that gives Flint more to say. It runs on your device.

## The short version

1. **Ask Flint.** Type what you want in plain words: "make a website for my band", "what should I do next".
2. **Bring an AI helper** when you want changes made for you. Open **Invite**, choose **Your AI agent**, or ask Flint "connect my AI".
3. **Ask for one thing.** "Add a contact section." Say what done looks like.
4. **Review it.** The helper proposes a change. You look, then approve or say what to change.
5. **Give Flint a brain on this device** if you want richer answers without sending anything away.

## Bring an AI helper, step by step

1. **Which AI do you already use?** Pick it. No AI yet? Pick **I don't have one**: Flint works with no AI, and there are two free options.
2. **What it may do.** Just answer me. Help build, and ask before big changes. Or work on its own on tasks you give it.
3. **Connect it.** Copy the one thing shown and paste it where the steps say.
4. **It says hello.** "Waiting for it to say hello" turns into a welcome. Give it a first job and watch the answer arrive in your private panel.

Find it later in your private panel, on the Agents page, or by typing @ and its name in a channel.

## Good to know

- You stay in charge. A helper acts as you and never above your own role. It can't change what it may do.
- Coming back? **Show me the setup again** connects the same helper. **Pause** stops it. **Remove** makes it leave.
- Private requests to your own agent stay between you and it.
- If an AI is not connected, the same buttons still work. You do the step yourself.

## If you get lost

Press **?** and pick **What should I do next?**, or ask Flint "I'm stuck".
