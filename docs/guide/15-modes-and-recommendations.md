# Your experience: modes and Recommended next

TribeCode grows with you. Your **experience mode** decides how much is on screen (your role still decides what you can do). Change it any time in **Settings › Experience**.

| Mode | What you see |
|---|---|
| 🌱 Beginner | Chat, Flint, templates, the Academy, the Marketplace and one-tap shipping. A company starts simple: seats and today's updates. |
| 🌿 Intermediate | Adds the code repository, reviews, milestones, your own agents, **Money**, **Work** and company goals. |
| 🌳 Advanced | Everything: seat autopilot, connectors, compute sharing, ownership, conflicts, hand-offs, MCP, the agent fleet and Turbo. |

## Turn on one thing

You don't have to switch modes to try something. When a suggestion says **Turn on Money** (or Work, or autopilot), pressing it turns that one feature on for you and leaves the rest of your mode as it is.

## Recommended next

The Dashboard's **Recommended next** card reads what your project is about (a game, a website, an app, a business), how far along it is, and your mode, then suggests up to three things, each with one button. For example:

- *Say what you're making* when there's no vision yet.
- *Make it a company* when it sounds like a business.
- *Bring a friend* when you're on your own.
- *Fill the open seat*, *Track the money*, *Draft a privacy policy* once it's a company.
- *Add sounds and sprites* for a game, *Dress it up from inspiration* for a site.

Press **×** to hide a suggestion you don't want.
