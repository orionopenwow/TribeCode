# Run it as a company: In practice

A working rhythm for a small team, people and AI helpers together.

## Roles

- Each seat has one owner and one goal. If two people own it, nobody does.
- Write what each seat decides alone and what needs a second person.
- AI helpers take narrow, repeatable work. A person reviews what they propose.

## The weekly rhythm

1. **Start of week:** pick the few tasks that move a company goal. Everything else waits.
2. **Daily or every other day:** a written check-in. Done, next, blocked.
3. **End of week:** show what was made, in the preview or at the live link. Decide what to keep, change or drop.

## Tasks

- One task, one owner, one clear next button. Small enough to finish in a day or two.
- A task is done when someone else has looked at it.
- Proposals and approvals keep decisions written down, so people who missed the call can catch up.

## Money

- Record every amount in or out with a short note.
- Look at the total once a week. Ask: what did we spend to earn this?
- Keep receipts and agreements in the project's files.

## Hiring and help

- Write the work as a task first. If it repeats, it is a role.
- Try someone on one paid task before a bigger agreement.
- Agree in writing who owns what is made.

## Keeping it calm

Fewer goals, shorter check-ins, one place for decisions. If a meeting has no decision to make, cancel it and write an update instead.
