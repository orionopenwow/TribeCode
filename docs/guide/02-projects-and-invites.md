# Make a project and invite friends

## Make a project

Tap **Create a project** on Home, or the **+** under the project circles. Then pick one:

- **Create my own**: a blank project.
- **A template**: Game, Website, App, Tool or Notes & research.
- **Bring my code** (host): a folder on this computer or a project on GitHub.

Give it a name (and a picture or a one-line bio if you like) and tap **Create**. The invite sheet opens next.

In a hurry? **Invite a friend** on Home makes a fresh project and copies a link in one go.

Each project gets its own chat, files, tasks and code. Change its name, emoji, color or picture in **Project settings** (Ctrl K, or the project menu at the top of the sidebar).

## Invite someone

Use **Invite friends** at the bottom of the sidebar, **Invite a friend** on a project's Dashboard, or the people button next to the bell.

The invite sheet shows:

- The link, already copied. Tap **Copy** again if you need to.
- **Share…** (on phones) and **QR code** for someone sitting next to you.
- **Connect my AI**, so your own agent joins too.

A link is good for 3 days and up to 10 people. A one-time guest pass works once and stops after a day.

## Make the link work from anywhere

Under the link it says where it works: **Works from anywhere**, **Same Wi-Fi only** or **This computer only**. If it isn't "anywhere", tap **Make it work from anywhere**. That turns on a public https address for your computer (see **The tribe's computer and sharing**).

## What your friend does

1. Taps the link.
2. Types a name under **Your name** and taps **Join the tribe**.
3. On a phone: taps **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**, so it opens like an app. (**Not now** skips it.)
4. On a computer: the app shows how to connect their own AI.

No account, no password, no download needed to join.

## Join someone else's tribe

Got a link? Tap the **+** under the circles and use **Paste invite link**, or open **Settings** and tap **Join another tribe with a link**.

## Delete a project

Host only: **Project settings** › **Delete this project…**, then type its name. Its chat, docs and tasks move to the tribe's trash folder. The code folder on your computer is not touched.
