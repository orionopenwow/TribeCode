# Put it on your phone

Five short steps take any app, game or website you make here to an icon on your phone's home screen. Open it from **Publish › Where it goes › Put it on your phone**, or tell Flint “how do I put my app on my phone”. Each step checks itself, says why it matters and what to do if you get stuck.

## 1. Make it installable

A phone only offers an icon when the page says the app's name, its icons and how to open. The check lists what is there and what is missing. **Make it installable** adds only what is missing: the app file (manifest), 192 and 512 pixel icons drawn from your Launch kit icon, a maskable icon for Android, an iPhone icon, the phone viewport and an offline copy. It is saved as a version, and **Undo** puts it back.

## 2. Put it online

Phones need a secure https link. A **GitHub page** is free and stays up when your computer is off; a **play link** is quick to try but stops when this computer sleeps; the **free cloud** keeps the whole tribe online and naps when nobody is there. **Check it** opens the live link and runs the install check on what phones will see. The free limits are listed with the page they come from.

## 3. Open it on your phone

Point the phone's Camera app at the code, or tap **Send by text**. The link has to open in Safari (iPhone) or Chrome or Samsung Internet (Android), not inside another app.

## 4. Add it to the home screen

- **iPhone, Safari:** Share, then Add to Home Screen, then Add. Since iOS 26 every site you add opens as a web app.
- **iPhone, other browsers:** the same, from their Share button (iOS 16.4 and later).
- **Android, Chrome:** the three dots, then Install app.
- **Inside Instagram, TikTok or Messenger:** open the link in Safari or Chrome first. Those apps' own browsers cannot add to the home screen.

## 5. A real store app (optional)

Most people stop at the home screen, which is free. A store app needs Apple's yearly developer membership or Google Play's one-time fee, and review. The Launch kit makes the screenshots, icon and store text. Expo's free plan includes a number of iOS and Android builds a month on its own machines, so you don't need your own Mac.

## With and without AI

Every step works with no AI. When your tribe has a stronger AI (your own Claude Code, Codex or Gemini), each step offers one ready-made ask, like “make my app work offline”. Your AI helpers work in their own corner, and you approve every change.
