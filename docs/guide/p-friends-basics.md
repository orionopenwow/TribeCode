# Work with friends: Basics

One link brings a friend into your project. They can join from a phone or a computer.

## The short version

1. **Invite.** Open your project and press **Invite**. Send the link or show the QR code.
2. **They join.** They tap the link, type a name and tap Join. On a phone they can add it to the Home Screen.
3. **Talk.** Use chat for quick notes and a call when you need to decide something.
4. **Plan.** Put work on the **Task Board**. Each task has one owner and one clear next button.
5. **Build together.** Edit docs and designs at the same time and see each other's changes live.
6. **Decide.** When something needs a yes or no, it shows as an approval you can answer with one tap.

## Good habits from day one

- Say what you are making in one sentence, so everyone builds the same thing.
- Small tasks. "Write the About section" beats "Do the website".
- If you talk about it in a call, write the decision in the project.

## What friends need

Nothing but the link. They do not need their own AI helper, and they do not need to install anything to start.

## Where things live

Everything stays on the computer of the person who started the tribe. See **Projects and invites** and **Phones** in the Guide's pages for the details.
