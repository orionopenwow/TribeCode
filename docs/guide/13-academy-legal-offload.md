# Academy, Legal and Offload

## Academy

**Academy** has short, plain-language lessons for every role: **Founder basics**, **CEO**, **Build**, **Design**, **Grow**, **Sales**, **Support**, **Run**, **Money** and **Legal**. 62 lessons, each with a *Try it in your tribe* step and a few trusted free readings.

If you hold a seat, the Academy opens on your role's track, and the seat's charter shows **Learn this role**. Progress and badges are kept for you.

## Legal

Ten plain-English starting templates: mutual NDA, contractor agreement, co-founder agreement, IP assignment, privacy policy, terms of service, cookie notice, service agreement (SOW), volunteer agreement and revenue share.

1. Pick a template and fill in the fields (required first; **More options** for the rest).
2. Watch the live preview, then **Create doc**. It lands in Files.
3. If the project is a company, the decision log notes it; you can add a *Send for signature* approval.

> Every document is a starting point, not legal advice. Have a lawyer in your jurisdiction review it.

## Offload

Some jobs don't need your paid AI. **Offload** (an Academy tab, or ask Flint "do this for free") suggests free options for the task, checked in November 2026: the Gemini app and Google AI Studio, ChatGPT Free, Mistral, OpenRouter's free models, and local models with Ollama or LM Studio.

1. Pick the task (research, a draft, a summary, a translation, image ideas, a code review, data cleanup).
2. **Copy the prompt** (it includes your project's name and goal, never your keys or secrets) and open the site.
3. Paste the answer into **Bring the result in**. It's saved to Files or Captures, labelled with where it came from.

Free tiers may use what you send to improve their models. Don't paste anything private.
