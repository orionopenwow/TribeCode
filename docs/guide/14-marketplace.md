# The Marketplace

515 checked add-ons for your project: sidebar widgets (Figma, Notion, Stripe, Plausible, Calendly and 100 more), Claude Code skills, libraries, connectors for your AI, creative studios like ArtCraft, playbooks, asset packs, site and company templates, legal templates and Academy tracks. Every link and license was checked; anything bundled is permissively licensed.

## Find things

Open **Workshop › Marketplace**, or **+ Add › Browse all** in the sidebar.

- **Search** by name or what it does.
- **Hot**, **Rising**, **New** and **Top** sort the list. Hot and Rising come from what *your tribe* used recently plus TribeCode's picks; there is no global chart.
- **Filters** opens one panel (kind, for games/sites/apps/businesses, seat, tags). Chosen filters show as chips you can clear.

## Use things

Each item has one button:

- **Add**: a widget goes into the project's sidebar; a library or skill goes into the Toolbox.
- **Use**: a playbook becomes a doc; a template starts your site or company.
- **Open**: a tool opens in a new tab.

When an AI helper asks to add something, a person approves it first.

## Make your own

Publish a **Module** from Workshop › Publish to share it with your tribe, then **Donate** to send it to every tribe through the community repository (see docs/MARKETPLACE.md).
