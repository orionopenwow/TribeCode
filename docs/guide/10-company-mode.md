# Run it as a company

Company mode turns a project into a small business with an org chart. Use it alone (you're the CEO and AI helpers take the other seats) or with friends (each person takes a role).

## Make a company

1. Open the project and pick **Company** in the sidebar. You can also tap **Make it a company** in *Recommended next* on the Dashboard.
2. Pick a template: **SaaS app**, **Agency**, **Creator**, **Game studio**, **Local business**, **Nonprofit**, **Club or class**, or **Blank**.
3. That's it. You get seats, a goal for each, one to three company goals and a few starter tasks on the Task Board.

New project? In setup, answer *What are you making?* with **A business**, and TribeCode makes the company and offers **Fill the other seats with AI helpers**.

## Seats

A seat is a job, not a title: **CEO**, **Build**, **Grow** and **Run** to start, and **+ Add seat** for Design, Sales, Support, Money, Legal or your own.

Tap a seat to open its charter:

- **This month**: the one goal for this seat.
- **Owns**: what it looks after (the website, the support inbox, a part of the code).
- **May do on its own** and **Needs your OK**: the line between doing and asking.
- **Weekly AI budget** and **Done means**.
- **Who holds it**: a person, an AI helper, or both ("Maya, backed by Maya's Claude").

An empty seat shows **Hire someone for this seat**, which posts a gig in **Work**.

## Today, goals and decisions

- **Today**: each seat posts a three-line update (done · next · blocked). AI helpers post theirs on their own; people get a one-tap form.
- **Goals**: one to three goals with a few numbers each. Tap **+1** as you make progress. The Dashboard's *At a glance* shows them.
- **Decisions**: every approval, vote and CEO call is kept with who decided and why, so nobody (and no AI) reopens settled questions.

Once a week you get **one** review in your Inbox (goals, what's waiting, what was spent). On the 1st, a one-page board update lands in Files.

## What stays human

Anything outward or costly asks a person first: spending money, publishing, emailing customers, contracts, shipping to production. Health, money and legal topics always go to a person. You can see every AI action in the audit trail.

## For your AI helper

In Claude Code, the TribeCode tools `my_seat`, `company_brief`, `seat_standup`, `seat_status`, `decisions_read` and `ceo_call` let your AI work its seat. A CEO call from an AI becomes an approval; it only counts once a person says yes.
