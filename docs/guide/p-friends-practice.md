# Work with friends: In practice

How a small group keeps moving without stepping on each other.

## Agree on three things

1. **The goal:** the one-sentence vision, and what "done for now" means.
2. **Who owns what:** split by area (words, design, build, testing). Owners decide in their area.
3. **How you decide:** a proposal with a short reason, then approvals. Silence is not a yes.

## Tasks

- Take a task before you start, so two people do not do the same thing.
- Move it forward with its next button. If you are blocked, say so on the task.
- A second person looks before it counts as done.

## Talking

- Chat for short questions. Use a thread or a direct message to keep the main room calm.
- Calls for decisions and for playing or reading through together. Captions and notes help whoever missed it.
- Comment on the page itself when the note is about one spot.

## Editing at the same time

- Docs and designs update live. You can see who is where.
- For files, work on your own task and let the owner or reviewer bring changes together.
- If two changes clash, the app says so. Talk first, then pick one.

## Reviews

Keep them kind and specific: what works, what to change, and why. Approve small things quickly so work does not pile up.

## Time zones and phones

- Write updates so someone reading later can act without asking.
- Turn on notifications for approvals and mentions only.
- The phone is good for chat, approvals and quick fixes; do long work on a computer.
