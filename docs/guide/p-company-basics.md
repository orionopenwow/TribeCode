# Run it as a company: Basics

Company mode puts roles, check-ins and simple money tracking around your project. Use it alone, with AI helpers in the other seats, or with friends.

## The short version

1. **Say what the company makes.** One sentence in the project's vision.
2. **Open Company** in the project's sidebar and answer a few short questions. You get seats, a goal for each seat and a few starter tasks.
3. **Fill the seats.** Give each role to a person, or to an AI helper.
4. **Do a short check-in.** What got done, what is next, what is in the way. A few lines each.
5. **Write down money in and out.** Even small amounts. It tells you if the idea pays for itself.
6. **Bring in help** when one seat has too much to do.

## You do not need much to start

- A solo company is fine. You hold every seat until you hand one over.
- Pick the template closest to you: app, agency, creator, game studio, local business, nonprofit, club or class, or blank.
- Three goals at most. One per seat is enough.

## What it is not

TribeCode does not register a business, hold money or give legal advice. It keeps your plan, roles and records in one place. See **Company mode** and **Money and Autopilot** in the Guide's pages for every screen.
