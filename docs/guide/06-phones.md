# Phones

A phone is a full member: chat, voice notes, approvals, tasks, docs and Ask AI. Only running code needs a computer.

## Install it like an app

After joining, the phone offers to put TribeCode on your Home Screen:

- **iPhone (Safari)**: tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**, then **Add**.
- **Android (Chrome)**: tap the **menu**, then **Install app**.

Open it from the Home Screen from then on. It stays signed in, and that's where notifications work (tap **Turn on notifications** when it asks).

Opened the link inside Messenger, Instagram or another app? A banner offers to open it in your real browser. Do that first, then add it to the Home Screen.

## Finding your way

- **Home** shows your projects and what's new. The **+** starts or joins one.
- In a project, the bottom bar has **Home** · **Chat** · **Files** · **Tasks** · **Agents**.
- **Swipe from the left edge** (or tap the flame logo) for your project circles and the section list.
- **Messages** on Home lists every chat and DM. **Talk to…** picks who you're talking to: Flint, your AI, the tribe's AI, your desktop, or #agents.
- Your avatar (top) opens your profile: invite, alerts, **Settings**, and this guide.

## Things phones are good at

- **Voice notes**: tap the mic, talk, send. Pause and delete are right there. It's transcribed.
- **Approvals**: tap **Approve** or **Deny** from the notification or the bell, with a comment if you like.
- **Ideas and tasks**: the **+** in the message box, or **New task** on Tasks.
- **Ask AI**: no AI of your own needed.

## Voice and notifications need https

Mic and push notifications only work over a secure link. If they don't, ask the host to turn on **Share beyond my Wi-Fi** (see **The tribe's computer and sharing**) and send a fresh invite.

## Sign a phone out

Each phone gets a private link that keeps it signed in. Treat it like a password. To sign one out: the **Devices & desktop app** button at the top of **Settings** (in the desktop app it says **Signed-in devices**).

## Phone-only friends

A friend with no computer and no AI subscription can still join, vote, talk, file bugs and ask **@ai**. Their code changes turn into requests a desktop picks up.
