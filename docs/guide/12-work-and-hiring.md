# Work: gigs, hiring, time and compute

**Work** (in the sidebar on Home, and in Ctrl K) is where people find vibecoding work, hire into the tribe, trade time and share spare AI. Everything stays inside your tribe unless you choose to share a gig publicly.

## Your work profile

Open your profile and press **Edit work profile**: skills, the seats you can fill, hours a week, how you like to be paid (*free*, *time exchange* or *paid*, in your own words), timezone, links, and **Open to work**.

## Gigs

1. **Post a gig**: a seat, a task or a bounty, with skills, hours and pay type.
2. People tap **I'm interested** and add a note.
3. Press **Hire**. A member goes straight into the seat; someone from outside gets an invite link and takes the seat when they join.

An empty company seat has **Hire someone for this seat**, which fills the gig in for you. AI helpers can draft gigs, but a person approves them first.

**Hot** and **New** sort the list; **Filters** narrows by skill, seat, pay type and hours.

## Time bank

Help someone for two hours, log it, and they confirm. Both sides must agree before hours count. Your balance shows hours given and received. No money changes hands.

## Compute

Offer your AI helper or your computer's local model to the tribe: what it is, hours a week, and your rules. Others press **Ask for time**; you get a message and an approval, and hours used count against your offer. Your subscription stays yours, and nothing is ever routed through your account without your yes.

## Public gigs

**Share publicly** prepares the gig for the community board (a public page on GitHub; you review the change before it goes up). Only the contact you choose is shared. **Public** shows gigs from other tribes.
