# The Launch kit

Everything the App Store and Google Play ask for, built in and free: store screenshots, an app icon, the store text, a paywall design, the launch checklist and the preview video step. Open it from **Publish › Where it goes › Launch kit**, from Ctrl K, or tell Flint “make store screenshots”.

## Screenshots

- Pick a **screen** for each screenshot: one captured from the live preview (its camera button), an App Creator screen, a picture you upload, or your paywall. A sample screen stands in until you have real ones.
- Type a **headline** and a second line, or tap one of 52 starters.
- Pick one of **50 styles**: different layouts, backgrounds, type and frames, in your brand colors.
- **Download** makes every size you tick at the exact pixels each store asks for: iPhone 6.9", 6.5" and 6.1", iPad 13", Google Play phone and tablet, and the 1024 × 500 feature graphic. The PNGs have no transparency, as Apple requires.

## Icon

Start from one of **54 styles** (a background, a symbol or your letters, a plate behind it), swap the colors, or use your own picture. You see it on light and dark home screens and in Android’s shapes. **Download the icons** gives every size: the 1024 px App Store icon, Google Play’s 512 px icon, Android’s adaptive layers and the web icons.

## Store text

Every field with its limit: App Store name, subtitle, keywords (counted in bytes), promotional text, description and what’s new; Google Play title, short and full description. Checks point out repeated words, keywords already in your name, wasted spaces, and claims the stores turn down, such as “best” or “#1”.

**Write a first draft** fills empty fields from your project’s name, vision and finished tasks, with no AI. **Make it better with AI** and **Translate with AI** need an AI turned on in Settings › AI. Nothing invents rankings, reviews or numbers.

## Paywall

17 layouts for your app’s subscription screen. Change the words, plans and button, then add it to your app, download it as a screen, or use it as a screenshot. Payments are not connected: the prices are examples for your own app.

## Checklist and preview video

The checklist lists each Apple and Google step in plain words, with a button to where you do it and room for notes. Ticks are saved with the project. The Preview video tab lists sizes and lengths, checks your YouTube link, and hands your screenshots to Motion Studio when it is in your app.

## Suggestions

When your tribe adds screens, posts release notes or repeats a keyword, the kit offers a one-tap fix you can undo.

Store rules change: the kit links Apple’s and Google’s pages as read on 15 November 2026. Read them again before you submit. This is not legal advice.
