# The Launch kit

Everything the App Store and Google Play ask for, built in and free: store screenshots, an app icon, the store text, a paywall design, the launch checklist and the preview video step. Open it from **Publish › Where it goes › Launch kit**, from Ctrl K, or tell Flint “make store screenshots”.

## Screenshots

- Pick a **screen** for each screenshot: one you captured from the live preview (the camera button), a screen from the App Creator, a picture you upload, or your paywall. Until you have real screens, a sample one stands in.
- Type a **headline** and a second line, or tap one of 52 starters.
- Pick one of **50 styles**. Each is a different layout, background, type and frame, in your brand colors.
- **Download** makes every size you tick, at the exact pixels each store asks for: iPhone 6.9", 6.5" and 6.1", iPad 13", Google Play phone and tablet, and the 1024 × 500 feature graphic. The files are PNGs with no transparency, which Apple requires.

## Icon

Start from one of **54 styles** (a background, a symbol or your letters, a plate behind it), swap the colors, or use your own picture. You see it on a light and a dark home screen and in the shapes Android uses. **Download the icons** gives every size: the 1024 px App Store icon, Google Play’s 512 px icon, Android’s adaptive foreground and background, and the web icons with a maskable one.

## Store text

Every field with its limit: the App Store name, subtitle, keywords (counted in bytes), promotional text, description and what’s new; the Google Play title, short and full description. Checks under each field point out words said twice, keywords already in your name, wasted spaces, and claims the stores turn down, such as “best” or “#1”.

**Write a first draft** fills the empty fields from your project’s name, vision and finished tasks, with no AI. **Make it better with AI** and **Translate with AI** need an AI turned on in Settings › AI. Nothing invents rankings, reviews or numbers.

## Paywall

17 layouts for your app’s subscription screen. Change the words, plans and button, then add it to your app (with the App Creator), download it as a screen, or use it as a screenshot. Payments are not connected: the prices are examples for your own app.

## Checklist and preview video

The checklist lists each step for Apple and for Google in plain words, with a button to the place where you do it and room for your own notes. Ticks are saved with the project. The Preview video tab lists the sizes and lengths, checks your YouTube link for Google Play, and hands your screenshots to Motion Studio when it is in your app.

## Suggestions

When your tribe adds new screens, posts release notes in chat, or repeats a keyword, the Launch kit offers a one-tap suggestion: refresh the screenshots, draft What’s New, or free up keyword space. Each one can be undone.

Store rules change. The kit paraphrases Apple’s and Google’s pages as read on 15 November 2026 and links to them; read them again before you submit. This is not legal advice.
