# Make a game: In practice

You have something playable. This is how to grow it without losing the fun.

## The loop

Work in short rounds: **pick one change, build it, play it, keep or undo.** Put each change on the Task Board as one task with one owner.

## What to build next, in order

1. **Feel.** Movement, jump, hit. Tune numbers until the basic action is satisfying with no goals at all.
2. **Goal and fail.** A score or a finish, and a clear way to lose. Restart must take under two seconds.
3. **Feedback.** A sound, a flash or a shake for every important event.
4. **Three levels or three minutes.** Enough to show the idea. Stop there and test.
5. **Menus last.** Title, pause, settings.

## Art and sound

- Pick one style and one small palette and stay in them.
- Keep pictures and sounds in the project's files with clear names: `player-run.png`, `hit.wav`.
- Only use art and sound you made or have permission to use. Note where each came from.

## Testing with people

- Publish a play link and send it. Do not explain the game. Watch where they get stuck.
- Write each problem as a bug or a task while you watch.
- Fix the first-minute problems before adding content.

## Working as a team

- Split by area: one person on play, one on art, one on levels.
- Use a call to play together and decide; write the decision in the project.
- Give AI helpers narrow tasks ("add a pause menu") and review what they propose before it goes in.

## Where it can go

In Publish, **Where it goes** offers what fits a game:

- **Play link** for quick tests while the tribe's computer is on.
- **A link that stays up**, through your own GitHub sign-in, for testers who play later.
- **itch.io**: a zip laid out the way itch wants it, with three steps. You upload it to your own itch.io account.
- **Install on phones**: an icon and an offline copy, from the lasting link.
- **Android app**: an app file built in your GitHub account, with a download link and a QR code.

Each publish is a version. Keep a line on what changed so testers know what to look at.
