# Make a game: Basics

A small game you can play in the preview today, and send to a friend as a link.

## The short version

1. **Say the idea in one sentence.** What you do, and why it is fun. "Dodge falling rocks and beat your best time."
2. **Pick a starter.** Open **Design** and choose a game starter. It already runs, so you begin from something playable.
3. **Change one thing at a time.** Ask Flint or your AI helper: "make the player faster", "add a score", "change the colors". Look at the preview after each change.
4. **Play it at phone size.** Use the phone size button in the preview. Can you play with thumbs? Is the text readable?
5. **Publish.** Press **Publish** to get a play link and a QR code. Under **Where it goes** you can also **Download it**, make **A link that stays up**, or get a zip laid out for **itch.io**.
6. **Invite testers.** Send the link to three people and watch what they do first.

## Keep it small

- One screen, one rule, one way to lose. Add more only after that is fun.
- Play it every few minutes. If a change makes it less fun, undo it.
- A game that takes 30 seconds to understand gets played. A long tutorial does not.

## With no AI

You can still pick a starter, open its files, change numbers and words, and publish. Flint answers questions and runs steps without an AI.

## If you get lost

Press **?** and pick **What should I do next?**, or ask Flint "what can I make".
