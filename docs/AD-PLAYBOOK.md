# Ad playbook

How TribeCode's short video ads are made, so every cut follows the same rules. The slow campfire cut
(`promo/ad-slow.html`, 33 s) is the reference. Its soundtrack is generated by `promo/sound/ad-soundtrack.py`.

## The rules we apply

1. **The first frame explains itself.** Most of an ad's impact lands in its first 2 seconds, and the first
   6 seconds carry most of the recall. So frame 0 already shows the product, the brand mark and one plain line:
   "Build apps with your friends and AI." There's no fade-in or logo sting first. The hook is the scene itself:
   friends around a campfire with laptops.
2. **One idea per beat, held long enough to read.** Every caption stays up for 4.5 seconds or more (most get
   5 to 7). Each beat has a number and a short verb, like "1 · Say it" or "2 · Build it together", so a viewer
   who joins late still knows where they are.
3. **Made for sound off.** The story is fully told by on-screen text: big centred captions (84 to 100 px at
   1080p), with the spoken idea typed out as a quote. Text is centred, not placed at the bottom like subtitles,
   where the platform UI covers it.
4. **Slow, eased camera.** The camera makes one deliberate move per beat: a slow push-in on Camp and the phone,
   then a gentle drift. Moves ease in and out over 1.2 s or more. Nothing cuts faster than the viewer can read.
5. **A story the viewer can follow.** The kombucha idea runs from start to finish:
   - the idea is said
   - friends' AI helpers build it in chat
   - the kombucha app runs in the preview
   - it becomes a company with one approval
6. **9:16 safe zones.** In the vertical cut, text stays out of the top 10–12%, the bottom 20–25% and the right
   15% (about 162 px), where TikTok, Reels and Shorts draw their buttons and captions. Characters and panels sit
   in the middle 60%.
7. **One CTA, held.** The end card shows a single action ("Start free at tribecode.org") with "Free and open
   source" under it, and holds for at least 4 seconds. There's no second link and no list of features.
8. **Length.** A 30 to 33 s cut for Meta Feed and YouTube. For TikTok and Reels, trim to 15–21 s by keeping
   beats 0, 1, 3 and the end card; each beat stands on its own.
9. **Sound.** Use original music only: no samples, and no trending audio without a license. The bed is warm and
   quiet (campfire crackle, pad, a light melody). Drums come in when the AI helpers start building and build
   slightly into the company beat. Small UI blips mark what you see happen. It resolves on the end card and fades
   out. Mix to about −16 LUFS integrated with true peak at −1.5 dBTP or below (ffmpeg two-pass `loudnorm`).
10. **Honest.** The UI in the ad is TribeCode's real UI and real features, and the app shown is one TribeCode
    could build. TribeCode prices never appear. Any numbers belong to the example kombucha business.

## Making a new cut

- Copy `promo/ad-slow.html`. Change the beat times in `BEATS` and the copy, keeping the rules above.
- Write frames with Playwright through `window.adSeek(t)` (deterministic), at 30 fps as JPEG q92.
- Encode with H.264: `-preset veryfast -crf 20 -pix_fmt yuv420p -movflags +faststart`.
- Generate the soundtrack: `python3 promo/sound/ad-soundtrack.py raw.wav`. Run `loudnorm` twice (measure, then
  linear). Mux with `-c:v copy -c:a aac -b:a 192k`.
- Check frame 0, every beat midpoint and the end card in both 1920×1080 and 1080×1920 before the full render.
