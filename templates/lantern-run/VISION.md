# Vision · Lantern Run

**One line:** a cozy, two-minute platformer about a small flame lighting up the night, built by a few friends
and their agents in one evening.

**Who it's for:** anyone with a keyboard or a phone and five spare minutes. No tutorial screens: the first
level teaches by itself.

**What makes it feel good**
- Movement first. Jumps forgive you (coyote time, jump buffering), and letting go early gives a short hop.
- The light is the clock. It slowly fades, embers bring it back, and the lantern floods the screen with
  light when you reach it. Tension without a timer on screen.
- Short levels you can finish in under a minute and want to replay faster.

**The first version (now):** three levels, embers, thorns, water, planks, bounce mushrooms, a title screen
and a win screen, sounds for every action.

**Not now:** enemies with AI, a level editor, save files, online leaderboards. Each could be a later
milestone if the tribe votes for it.

**How we build it:** each folder is a lane (README.md). One role per lane, small changes, a gist after
every step, and a vote in chat before anything changes this vision.
