# Vision · Tavern 3D

**One line:** a warm little room full of characters that feel alive, and a test bed for *agents living in a room*.

**Why it exists:** TribeCode is about people and their Claude Codes building together. The tavern makes that
visible. Each person, or their Claude Code, owns a patron or a system (the fire, the music, the menu, the
weather outside), and the room shows what everyone's work does when it runs side by side.

**Rules of the room**
- Patrons are bots and always say so: a BOT tag over every head and in the Patrons panel. Never passed off
  as players.
- Each patron has a role (barkeep, bard, cook, regulars) and a simple life: go to their spot, work or sit,
  wander, chat with each other, react when you come near.
- People can give simple orders: Follow me, Stay, Go to your spot.
- Data over code: names, roles, homes and lines live in `src/npcs/patrons.json`, so friends and agents add
  patrons without touching the engine.

**The first version (now):** one room, six patrons, talk bubbles, first-person walking, the Patrons panel.

**Next, if the tribe votes for it:** patrons that remember you, a menu and orders at the bar, music from the
bard, a second room upstairs, a patron driven live by a teammate's Claude Code.

**Original only:** our own names, rooms and characters. Nothing from commercial games.
