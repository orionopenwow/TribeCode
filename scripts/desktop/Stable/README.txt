STABLE  (the installed app, released versions)
==============================================

Double-click Update-and-Open-TribeCode.bat.

- Installs TribeCode the first time, updates the .exe to the newest release, and opens it.
- The .exe only changes when you double-click this file, or press "Restart to update"
  inside the app (it may download quietly in the background, but it never installs on its own).
- Uses your own tribe: %USERPROFILE%\TribeCode
- Releases come from main: new work is tried in ..\Working first, then released.
