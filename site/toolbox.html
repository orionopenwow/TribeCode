// what free means here

Free where it counts

  • Everything on your computer. Projects, chat, docs, files, tasks, Company mode, Flint and the design canvas.
  • Open source. Read the code, change it, run your own copy.
  • Your own AI. The AI helper you already use, a key you bring, or a free model on your computer.

If paid plans come later, they will be for things that cost money to run for you, like always-on hosting. They will never take a feature away from your own computer.
