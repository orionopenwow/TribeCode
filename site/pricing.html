Pricing

TribeCode is free, with everything in it. Paid extras like hosting will come later, and only once they work well.

  • Free stays free. Everything free today stays free: self-hosting, unlimited friends and projects, bringing your own AI.
  • Your code is yours. It lives in your GitHub repo, MIT licensed. Leave any time and take everything.
  • We charge only for what costs us money: servers that stay on, backups, and AI we run for you.
  • No surprise bills. You see the cost before a big AI job, and spending stops at the cap you set.
  • No ads, and we never sell your data.
