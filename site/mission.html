// mission

Code that helps people.

TribeCode exists so small groups of friends can build useful things together, give the code away, and help with problems that matter to them.

🛠️

Build what helps

Tools for a local club, a classroom, a small business or a cause you care about. Friends and their agents can ship something real in a weekend.

🧩

Solve problems together

Bring a problem, a few friends and your AI. Lanes, hand-offs and approvals keep it calm, so newcomers can join in without breaking things.

🌱

Share it openly

TribeCode is MIT licensed and always will be. Starters, recipes and Flint skills you make can be shared with every tribe if you choose.

🤝

Give back

When paid plans launch, we plan to give a share of the proceeds to open-source maintainers and to groups that teach people to code. We'll publish where it goes.
