// mission

One place to build, run and work together.

TribeCode is where friends, and the AI helpers they already use, make useful apps, games and websites, turn them into real businesses, and find people to work with. Code, AI and vibecoding in one calm room, free and open source, so anyone can start with a sentence and a link.

🛠️

Build what helps

Tools for a local club, a classroom, a small business or a cause you care about. Describe it, watch it run in the preview, and steer it in plain words. Friends and their agents can ship something real in a weekend.

🏢

Run it as a company

Give the work around your product a shape: seats like CEO, Build, Grow and Run, held by friends or AI helpers, with short charters, daily check-ins and real numbers. A one-person C-suite or a crew of friends.

🤝

Find work, hire, trade time

Post a gig, hire someone straight into a seat, trade hours with other builders, or share spare AI time. Vibecoding is real work now, and TribeCode helps people find it and get credit for it.

🎓

Learn every role

Plain-language lessons for code and for running a business: marketing, sales, support, money, legal basics. Learn by doing it in your own tribe, at your own pace.

🌱

Share it openly

TribeCode is MIT licensed and always will be. Starters, templates, modules and skills you make can go to every tribe through the Marketplace if you choose.

💛

Give back

When paid plans launch, we plan to give a share of the proceeds to open-source maintainers and to groups that teach people to code. We'll publish where it goes.

// new in v0.27 · company mode

Run the business around it

Useful things need someone to look after them. Company mode lets one person or a whole crew run what they build: seats like CEO, Build, Grow and Run go to friends or AI helpers, each with a short charter, and short check-ins arrive on their own.

See Company mode · Learn: from project to business · Read the docs

// what stays human

People approve anything outward or costly: money, publishing, customer emails, contracts. Legal documents are starting points to check with a professional, and every number shows where it came from.
