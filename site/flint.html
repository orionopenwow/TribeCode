Meet Flint

TribeCode's own helper: it runs the app for you, teaches you, and works without a paid AI.

You ask in plain words, like you'd ask a friend. Flint opens things, makes changes, writes things down and remembers how you like to work. Below is the real app, playing by itself.

// what flint does

One helper, the whole app

Eight things Flint does for you every day. Each one plays out below in the real app.

01 · runs the app

Runs the app for you

What this means for you: you never have to hunt for a button. Say where you want to go, and Flint takes you there and shows you where it lives.

  1. You ask: “open the task board”
  2. Flint opens it and lights up where it lives
  3. Then the whiteboard, then an invite for Sam

Try it: click the rows in the sidebar.

02 · builds with you

Builds anything you can describe

What this means for you: describe an app in one sentence and a first version is running on your phone minutes later, built by Flint and your AI helpers.

  1. You describe it in one sentence
  2. Flint and your AI helpers write the code
  3. It runs on your phone, and keeps improving
03 · teaches your way

Teaches you the way you learn

What this means for you: no confusing jargon. Ask what anything means and get it as a picture, a comparison, code or simple steps, whichever clicks for you.

  1. You ask: “teach me what an API is”
  2. Pick a picture, an analogy or code
  3. A quick quiz, if you like. Flint remembers your style
04 · writes your docs

Writes your docs

What this means for you: the boring writing gets done. Launch plans, read-me pages, meeting notes and change lists, written from what your tribe already said and did.

  1. You ask: “write the launch doc”
  2. Flint reads your plan, tasks and chat
  3. A draft appears in Files for you to check
05 · knows you

Remembers you, and knows your tribe

What this means for you: Flint greets you with what changed and what to do next, and remembers what you tell it. Only you can see those notes, and you can delete them any time.

  1. You say good morning
  2. Flint catches you up and suggests what's next
  3. “Remember…” saves a private note you can edit
06 · any brain, or none

Works with no AI, or with any AI

What this means for you: Flint is free and works straight away, even on a phone with no signal. When you want more, plug in an AI: one that runs on your own computer, or one the host already has.

  1. No AI: about 170 everyday actions, free builds and how-to answers
  2. A free AI on your device or the host's computer
  3. Or a cloud AI, with the host's own key
07 · learns new tricks

Learns your tribe's shortcuts

What this means for you: things you do often become one word. Flint notices a routine and turns it into a shortcut the whole tribe can use.

  1. You do the same few things again
  2. Flint offers to save them as /ship-friday
  3. Anyone runs all the steps with one word
08 · safe by design

Asks before anything that matters

What this means for you: nothing happens behind your back. Spending money, publishing or emailing customers always waits for a person to say yes.

  1. An AI helper wants to email 120 customers
  2. It waits for a person's OK
  3. Camp taps Approve, and only then it goes

// questions

Good to know

What can Flint do without any AI?

A lot: he understands about 170 everyday actions across the app (tasks, messages, the calendar, themes, docs, the design canvas, websites and more), handles typos and several steps in one message, answers how-to questions from the Guide, learns phrases you teach him and builds sites, starters and design screens from templates. Open-ended writing, reasoning about code and free conversation need a model.

Does Flint cost money?

No. Flint is part of TribeCode, which is free and open source. It needs no AI to do the everyday things. If you add one, a model in your browser or on the host's computer is free; a cloud model uses the host's own account.

What does Flint remember, and who can see it?

Only what you ask it to remember, like your name or how you like to learn. Only you can read those notes. You can edit or delete them in Settings › Flint, and they're never part of anyone else's saved copy.

Does Flint read my private messages?

No. Flint works from the project you're in: tasks, docs, the plan and the channels you can see.

Which AI does Flint use?

None by default. You can choose one that runs in your browser, a free one on the host's computer (like Qwen through Ollama), or a cloud AI the host connects. Your own agent (Claude, Cursor, Codex, ChatGPT, Gemini and more) joins separately: you invite it like a friend, and it gets your access and never more.

Can Flint do things without asking?

Only small things inside your own app, the same things your buttons do. Spending, publishing and emailing people always wait for a person's OK, and shortcuts follow the same rules.
