// questions

Good to know

Does Flint cost money?

No. Flint is part of TribeCode, which is free and open source. In basic mode it needs no AI at all. If you give it a brain, the browser model and Ollama are free; a cloud model uses the host's own key.

What does Flint remember, and who can see it?

Only what you ask it to remember, like your name or how you like to learn. The notes live with your tribe, only you can read them, you can edit or delete them in Settings › Flint, and they're never part of anyone else's saved copy.

Does Flint read my DMs?

No. Flint works from the room you're in: tasks, docs, the project's vision and the channels you can see.

Which AI does Flint use?

None by default. You can choose a model that runs in your browser, the host's Ollama (Qwen and friends), or a cloud model the host connects with their own key. Your own Claude Code, Codex or Gemini join as separate helpers.

Can Flint do things without asking?

Only small things inside your own app, the same things your buttons do. Spending, publishing and emailing people always wait for a person's OK, and skills follow the same rules.
