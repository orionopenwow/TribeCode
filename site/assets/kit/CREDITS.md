# Credits for the homepage asset-library preview

These files show what TribeCode's asset library (templates/assets.json) brings into a project.

## Sounds and pixel sprites
`jump.wav`, `coin.wav`, `powerup.wav`, `laser.wav`, `hit.wav`, `explosion.wav` and the `*.svg` sprites were made by
TribeCode's own sound and sprite kit (`server/assetkit.js`). CC0-1.0.

## 3D models (turntable renders, `m-*.webp`)
Rendered from the Khronos glTF Sample Assets (github.com/KhronosGroup/glTF-Sample-Assets).
- Lantern, Avocado, BoomBox, WaterBottle: Microsoft. CC0-1.0.
- Fox: model by PixelMannen (CC0-1.0); rigging and animation by tomkranis (CC-BY-4.0).
- RiggedFigure, BoxAnimated: Cesium. CC-BY-4.0.

## Textures (`t-*.webp`)
Example swatches drawn for this preview, not taken from Poly Haven. CC0-1.0. In the app, texture search uses
Poly Haven's CC0 library.
