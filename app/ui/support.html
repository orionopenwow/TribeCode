Support

Stuck, found a bug, or worried about something someone posted? Write to support@tribecode.org. A person reads every message. For tribes we run (the demo and featured tribes) we act on reports within 24 hours; every other tribe is run by its host, and we help you reach them.

Someone is being abusive

In the app, long-press a message (or tap a profile's "…") and choose Report or Block. Reports go straight to the tribe's host and moderators. Blocking hides their messages and notifications for you everywhere.

Report a problem

In the app: Help › Send feedback goes to your tribe's own bug inbox. To reach us instead, fill this in; it opens your email app with the details.

We add your browser and device type so we can reproduce it. Nothing else is sent.
