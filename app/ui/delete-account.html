Delete your TribeCode account

For the TribeCode app on the web, desktop, iPhone and Android

Do it yourself, in the app

  1. Open TribeCode and go to Settings › Privacy & safety.
  2. Tap Delete my account…, type DELETE and confirm.

This removes your account from your tribe: your profile, sign-ins and devices, links to Discord, GitHub or Google, your private notes, and the messages, uploads and docs you posted (deleted, or shown as "deleted member" where a thread would stop making sense). It also clears everything TribeCode keeps on that device. It happens at once and can't be undone.

If you only used TribeCode on this device (no tribe, or "On this device"), the same button clears everything here.

Who holds your data

TribeCode is self-hosted. Your tribe's data lives on its host's computer or server, not with the TribeCode project. The host can also remove you and everything you posted. Code you pushed to a Git repository stays in that repository's history unless its owner rewrites it. See the privacy policy.

No longer have the app? Ask us

Send this and we will delete it for tribes we run (the demo and featured tribes) within 30 days, and help you reach the host of any other tribe. We only use what you send to handle the request.

This opens your email app with the request to support@tribecode.org.
