Privacy

Privacy policy

Draft of 10 November 2026 · applies to the TribeCode web app, desktop app and planned phone apps

The short version

  • TribeCode is self-hosted. Your tribe's chat, tasks, docs, files and accounts are stored on the host's computer or on a server the host chose. The TribeCode project does not run a central server that receives them.
  • We (the TribeCode project) do not sell data, show ads, or track you across apps and websites.
  • Optional services (GitHub, Discord or Google sign-in, an AI provider you pick, a tunnel) only get data when you or your host turn them on.
  • You can delete your account from inside the app: Settings › Privacy & safety › Delete my account.

Who is responsible for what

A tribe is run by its host: the person whose computer or server keeps it online. The host controls the data in their tribe and decides who can join. The TribeCode project makes the software and, for the phone apps, publishes them in the app stores. When this policy says "we", it means the TribeCode project; when it says "the host", it means the person running your tribe.

Optional services

These only receive data when you or your host turn them on. Each has its own privacy policy.

  • GitHub: signing in, pushing and pulling the project's code, and showing repository pictures.
  • Discord, GitHub or Google sign-in: your name, email address and avatar from that account, to sign you in. You can sign in without them using an invite link.
  • AI models: when you ask an AI, your question and the context TribeCode adds (for example recent chat or a file) go to the model you picked: one running in your browser or on the host's computer (nothing leaves the device), a teammate's Claude Code (with their permission), or a cloud provider such as Anthropic, OpenAI or Google using a key that you or the host added. Before anything from your device first goes to a cloud provider, TribeCode asks once, naming the provider; the answer is remembered on that device and you can revoke it in Settings › Privacy & safety. Models in your browser or on the host's computer don't ask.
  • Reaching the host from anywhere: Tailscale or Cloudflare tunnels carry the traffic between your device and the host.
  • Push notifications: your browser's or phone's push service (Apple, Google, Mozilla) delivers notifications; the text is encrypted for your device.
  • Link previews, GIF search (GIPHY) and embedded videos: the host's server fetches them, so your device doesn't contact those sites directly.
  • Downloads: the in-browser AI model, the 3D viewer (three.js) and some fonts load from public CDNs (jsDelivr, Hugging Face) when you first use them.

Phone permissions

The phone apps ask only when you use the feature: the microphone for voice notes and huddles, the camera to scan an invite QR code or take a picture, notifications for approvals and mentions, and Face ID / fingerprint if you lock the app. Everything still works if you say no, except that feature.

Deleting your data

  • In the app: Settings › Privacy & safety › Delete my account. This removes your account and what you posted from the tribe (when your tribe's server supports it) and clears everything on your device.
  • If you no longer have the app: use the account deletion page: it explains the in-app steps and sends us a request (we handle tribes we run within 30 days and help you reach the host of any other tribe).
  • Code you pushed to a Git repository stays in that repository's history unless its owner rewrites it.

Children

TribeCode is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). Hosts should not invite them without a parent's consent.
