The software

TribeCode is open-source software under the MIT licence. You may run it, change it and share it under that licence. It comes as is, without warranty.

Tribes and hosts

Each tribe is run by its host, who keeps its data and decides who joins. By joining a tribe you agree to that tribe's own rules, and to these terms.

What you post

  • You own what you write and make. You let the members of your tribe see and use it inside the tribe and its projects.
  • Don't post anything illegal, hateful, harassing, sexually explicit, violent or threatening, spam, malware, or anything that infringes someone else's rights. There is no tolerance for objectionable content or abusive users.
  • Anyone can report a message or a person and block people. The host and mods review reports and can delete messages, mute or remove members. We aim for reports to be looked at within 24 hours in tribes we host or feature.
  • The host can filter words from chat.

AI helpers and code

  • AI agents only change your project after a person approves it, and they work in your project's repository, not on your phone. You are responsible for checking what you merge, run and publish.
  • The phone apps do not download or run code that changes the app itself. Projects you build may be previewed in a sandboxed web view.
  • AI answers can be wrong. Don't rely on them for safety-critical decisions.

Accounts

You can delete your account at any time in Settings › Privacy & safety. Hosts can remove members who break these terms.

Purchases

TribeCode is free. The phone apps sell nothing. If that changes, purchases in the iPhone app will use Apple's in-app purchase.
